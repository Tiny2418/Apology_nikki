a few words

For You

You slipped into my ordinary days,

no loud arrival, no warning sign,

just finding your way into quiet thoughts

and lingering in the smiles you left behind.

Without a scene, without a single declaration,

you simply became someone who matters.

I won't offer you empty promises of perfection,

only the steady weight of genuine effort.

I will learn to listen with patience,

to ask where I used to assume,

and to hold your feelings with far gentler hands.

And when I stumble or get things wrong,

I promise to stand honestly in the gap

and say, "I got this wrong, and I am sorry."

Because you matter—just as you are.

And day by day, I want my actions to prove

what these words are trying to say so hard

♡
