a letter

I’m sorry, Nikku.

I don't know every single detail of where I went wrong, and I'm not going to pretend I have it all figured out.

Maybe there were times I should have spoken up about you, or moments I should have reached out and asked first. Maybe I was just careless and failed to pause and consider how my choices would affect you.

Whatever the exact moments were, seeing that I hurt you breaks my heart. And for that, I am truly, deeply sorry.

I know a simple apology doesn't fix what happened. I’m not saying this to hide behind excuses or to ask you to pretend it didn't happen. I care about you more than I can express, and this bond means a truly priceless to me. It is painful to know I was careless with someone who matters so much to me.

Going forward, I want to ask instead of making assumptions. I want to truly hear you instead of trying to justify myself. When I make a mistake, I want to own it, learn from it, and do better.

I can’t promise I’ll be perfect, but I can promise you real effort, total honesty, and my complete commitment to treating you with the love and respect you deserve.

I am so sorry for ever making you feel like you were anything less than cherished.

— Tiny ♡
